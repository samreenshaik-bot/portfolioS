About me

With 3+ years of experience in the digital space, I’ve explored digital marketing, brand building, e-commerce, content creation and business development. My journey didn’t start with a plan. It started with curiosity and one simple question:

“I have an idea. How can I make it work?”

The art of noticing

I’ve always had a creative way of looking at things. I notice details, connect ideas, identify opportunities and think about how things can be done differently. I call it my art of noticing.

I don’t believe you need to know everything before starting something. I started exploring business at 16, faced failures, tried again and learned along the way. I didn’t initially know graphic design, content creation or digital marketing. I learned through YouTube tutorials, online research, experimentation and reaching out to experts.

My biggest strength isn’t knowing everything. It’s having the confidence to take responsibility, learn what I need and find a way to make things work.

From ideas to execution

I’ve gained experience in digital marketing, social media management, Meta advertising, creative campaigns and business operations. At Berkshire International Services and Razan&Co, I developed my marketing and content skills and gained exposure to media metadata through projects involving Warner Bros. and McClatchy.

With Hoverup, my clothing brand, I explored the business side firsthand, from building the brand and its Shopify website to creating content and running marketing campaigns. Working in MNC environments has also given me experience communicating with international clients, particularly from the United States.

What I do today

I bring together digital marketing, performance marketing, creative thinking and business growth. I enjoy identifying opportunities, understanding audiences, building brands and turning ideas into practical solutions. Creativity gets you thinking, confidence gets you started, and consistency helps you make things happen.
